================================================================================
                         TECHNICAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
  - Translate transliterated Akkadian cuneiform (2000 BC) to English
  - Low-resource NMT: Only ~8,000 training examples
  - Morphologically complex language
  - Heavily formatted/noisy text (scribal marks, determinatives, gaps)

SOLUTION APPROACH:
  1. Robust preprocessing pipeline (handles 20+ formatting issues)
  2. Transfer learning (mBART-50, mT5 pre-trained models)
  3. Data augmentation (back-translation for 2x training data)
  4. Morphology-aware architectures (character + subword tokenization)
  5. Multi-task learning (translation + auxiliary tasks)
  6. Ensemble of diverse models

EXPECTED PERFORMANCE:
  Baseline Seq2Seq:        BLEU ~15   → Geometric Mean ~21
  Pre-trained mBART:       BLEU ~20   → Geometric Mean ~28
  + Augmentation:          BLEU ~23   → Geometric Mean ~31
  + Advanced Models:       BLEU ~25   → Geometric Mean ~33
  + Ensemble (Final):      BLEU ~27   → Geometric Mean ~35

EVALUATION:
  Primary: Geometric Mean of BLEU × chrF++ (competition metric)
  Secondary: Proper noun accuracy, determinative accuracy
